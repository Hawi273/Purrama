Meet Mochi

The cat from the logo, awake and sitting up. Mochi is one SVG and one class: swap pm--wave for pm--hop and he changes what he's doing. Use one per screen, in empty states, loading, success and errors.

Idle pm--idle Default resting state
Blink pm--blink Hover on a card, small reactions
Sleep pm--sleep Empty watchlist, night mode
Tail swish pm--tail Footer, about page
Wave hello pm--wave Welcome, sign in, onboarding
Curious pm--curious No search results, help
Happy hop pm--hop Success, saved, signed up
Star burst pm--rated Right after rating a film
Love pm--love Added to favourites
Look around pm--look While a search runs
Loading pm--loading Fetching films
Peek-a-boo pm--peek 404, page corners
Sad pm--sad Errors, offline

Stargazing pm--night Hero, late-night picks
